\textbf{Question:} 

\begin{align*}
\vec{L} = 
\myvec{
3 & -1 & -1 & -1 \\
-1 & 2 & -1 & 0 \\
-1 & -1 & 3 & 0 \\
-1 & 0 & -1 & 1
}
\end{align*}

Which of the following are TRUE? 


\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
\item The matrix $\vec{L}$ is row equivalent to 
\myvec{
0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
-1 & 2 & -1 & 0 \\
-1 & -1 & 3 & 0 \\
-1 & 0 & -1 & 1
}

\item The linear system $\vec{L}\vec{x} = \vec{b}$ has a solution for all $\vec{b}$

\item For $\vec{b} = 
\myvec{
1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \\ 1
}$, the system $\vec{L}\vec{x} = \vec{b}$ has a solution

\item Rank of the matrix $\vec{L}$ is $3$
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Solution:}\\

Given:
\begin{align} 
\vec{L} = 
\myvec{
3 & -1 & -1 & -1 \\
-1 & 2 & -1 & 0 \\
-1 & -1 & 3 & 0 \\
-1 & 0 & -1 & 1
} 
\end{align}

\newpage

Row reduction (Option a):
\begin{align}
R_1 \rightarrow{R_1 + R_2 + R_3 + R_4}\implies 
\vec{L} &\sim
\myvec{
0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
-1 & 2 & -1 & 0 \\
-1 & -1 & 3 & 0 \\
-1 & 0 & -1 & 1
} 
\end{align}


 Rank of  $\vec{L}$ (Option d):
 \begin{align}
\text{One row is dependent, remaining 3 independent} \implies \text{rank}(\vec{L}) = 3
\end{align}

System  $\vec{L} \vec{x} = \vec{b}$ for all  $\vec{b}$ (Option b): 
\begin{align}
\text{Rank}(\vec{L}) = 3 < 4 \implies \text{Solution will not be there for all b}
\end{align}

Dependence property(Option c):
 \begin{align}
R_1 + R_2 + R_3 + R_4 &= \vec{0} \\
\implies (R_1 + R_2 + R_3 + R_4)\vec{x} &= 0 \\
\implies R_1\vec{x} + R_2\vec{x} + R_3\vec{x} + R_4\vec{x} &= b_1 + b_2 + b_3 + b_4 = 0 \\
\text{For } \vec{b} = \myvec{1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \\ 1}, \quad b_1+b_2+b_3+b_4 &= 4 \neq 0 \implies \text{no solution}
\end{align}
